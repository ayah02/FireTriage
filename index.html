How It Works

From fire telemetry to
tactical decision in seconds

Three inputs. Four horizons. One ranked triage queue — so you always know which fire demands attention right now.

01
Enter Fire Telemetry

Distance to evac boundary (meters), closing velocity (m/h), and fuel-wind alignment (0–1). Three inputs is all the model needs. Enter them in under 30 seconds.

02
ML Pipeline Runs

XGBoost + LightGBM + Ridge Classifier ensemble fires instantly. Temperature scaling (12H) and isotonic regression (24/48/72H) calibration ensures the percentages are meaningful — not just model guesses.

03
Take Action

See calibrated breach probabilities at 12, 24, 48, and 72 hours. Ask the AI copilot to draft evacuation orders, explain a risk score, or recommend resource deployment — in plain firefighter language.
