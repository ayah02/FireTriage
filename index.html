Capabilities
Built for the EOC

Every feature designed around the real operational needs of an Emergency Operations Center.

📡

Real-Time Incident Ingestion

Log fire incidents with geolocation, fire distance, closing speed, and alignment vector via the sidebar form. Triage queue auto-sorts by 48h probability.

🗺️

Geospatial Tactical Map

Plotly Scattermapbox on carto-darkmatter tiles. Each incident is plotted with risk-color markers. Click any marker to drill into that incident's analytics.

⏱️

Multi-Horizon Predictions

Simultaneous 12h, 24h, 48h, and 72h fire spread probability curves with monotonicity enforcement ensuring P(t₁) ≤ P(t₂) for all t₁ < t₂.

🤖

AI Tactical Copilot

Embedded LLM chat with full incident context. Groq LLaMA-3.3-70b primary → OpenRouter fallback → offline rule-based engine. Wildfire-scoped responses only.

📊

Gauge + Probability Cards

48h Plotly gauge dial with color-coded risk zones plus individual probability cards for each forecast horizon with percentage and classification badge.

⚡

Live Feed Simulation

Activate real-time feed mode to simulate incoming incident updates. Watch probabilities and triage rankings update dynamically as conditions evolve.
