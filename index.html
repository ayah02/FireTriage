What It Does
Everything an Emergency Operations Center needs in one screen.
📡

Incident Ingestion

Log fires with location, distance, speed, and wind alignment. Queue auto-sorts by 48h probability.

🗺️

Tactical Map

Plotly Scattermapbox on dark tiles. Risk-colored markers per incident.

📊

Multi-Horizon Predictions

12h · 24h · 48h · 72h spread probabilities with monotonicity enforcement.

🤖

AI Copilot

Groq LLaMA-3.3-70b with full incident context. Falls back to OpenRouter then offline engine.

⚡

Live Simulation

Real-time feed mode updates incident data and recalculates risk rankings dynamically.

🎚️

Calibrated ML

Temperature Scaling (12h) and Isotonic Regression (24/48/72h) from the V31 ensemble.
